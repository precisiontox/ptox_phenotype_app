Endpoint distribution ? Compares the distribution of values for the selected endpoint across all chemicals between controls and chemical exposures.

Chemical ranking

?
Ranks chemicals using a response summary metric for the selected endpoint. Only non-control numeric endpoint values are included. The options are:
  • Mean response: mean endpoint value across all tested concentrations for each chemical
  • Max. response: maximum recorded endpoint value across all tested concentrations for each chemical
  • Response at max. concentration: mean endpoint value at the highest tested concentration for each chemical
  • Empirical PoD: lowest tested concentration whose mean response departs from the matched control by at least 2 control standard deviations, or 20% of the control mean when control variability is unavailable

Dose-response curves ? Shows endpoint response against exposure concentration for the selected chemical, grouped by exposure duration.
By default, all batches are pooled into a single curve: conditions at the same concentration are treated as replicates, and the control line is the mean of the controls from those batches.
Enable "Separate curve per batch" to draw one line per experiment batch (for both exposures and controls).
